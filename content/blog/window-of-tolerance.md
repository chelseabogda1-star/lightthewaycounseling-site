Want to learn more about how our bodies respond to our environment? Or maybe how your body has responded to a traumatic event? Join our masters level intern, Sarah Howell, as she walks you through a mental health concept referred to as the Window of Tolerance.

This concept can be extremely helpful in understanding why our bodies respond to stressful stimuli. It can also help act as a starting point to recognizing what it is that throws us off balance as well as coping skills we can use to get us back to our equilibrium.
