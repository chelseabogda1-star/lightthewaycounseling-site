## What is Self-Esteem?

Self-esteem is the cornerstone of mental health, encompassing the beliefs and attitudes we hold about ourselves. It's not merely about feeling good; it's about recognizing your inherent value, capabilities, and potential. When nurtured, healthy self-esteem serves as a resilient buffer against life's challenges, enabling you to navigate setbacks with resilience and grace.

## Impacts of low Self-Estem

The repercussions of low self-esteem reverberate across every aspect of life. From strained relationships and diminished performance to heightened anxiety and depression, its effects can be far-reaching. Left unaddressed, low self-esteem can undermine your ability to pursue your goals and lead a fulfilling life.

## Common Symptoms

Recognizing the signs of low self-esteem is the first step toward reclaiming your confidence. Symptoms may manifest as persistent self-doubt, excessive self-criticism, fear of failure, and a tendency to prioritize others' needs over your own. You may find yourself trapped in a cycle of negative self-talk, unable to appreciate your strengths and achievements.
